HA Backup Privacy Policy

HA Backup is a private, personal-use connection between the operator's Home Assistant backup system and the operator's Google Drive account. It is not a public application or commercial service.

Google Drive access

With the account owner's authorization, HA Backup may upload, locate, verify, and retrieve protected Home Assistant backup archives in Google Drive. The connection may access file names, IDs, sizes, and other Drive metadata needed for those operations.

Backup contents and credentials

Home Assistant backup archives may contain household configuration and other personal information. The intended workflow transfers protected backup files without decrypting them. OAuth credentials are kept in the operator's privately managed systems and are not published on this website.

Sharing and retention

The application does not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Files are stored in the owner's selected Google Drive account and are subject to Google's applicable service and privacy policies. Backup retention and deletion are controlled by the operator.

Website visitors

This static website does not include its own analytics, advertising, sign-in forms, or tracking scripts. It is hosted through GitHub Pages, whose hosting platform may process ordinary website connection data.

Access and revocation

The Google account owner can review or revoke the application's access at Google Account connections.
